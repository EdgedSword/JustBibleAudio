# JustBibleAudio

The King James Bible read aloud, for the JustBible app.

**The audio is not in this repository.** It is attached to
[Releases](../../releases) — one archive per book, downloaded by the app a
book at a time. Nothing here is worth cloning; the repository exists so the
releases have somewhere to live.

## What is there

| | |
|---|---|
| 66 book archives | 31,102 verses, one file per verse |
| `Headings.zip` | 1,189 spoken chapter headings and the numerals 1–176 |
| Total | about 1.5 GB |

AAC at 40 kbps, 24 kHz mono. Psalms is the largest book at 97 MB; 3 John is
the smallest at under 1 MB.

## The text

The [Pure Cambridge Edition](https://www.bibleprotector.com) of the King
James Bible. Verse numbers are not spoken. Psalm superscriptions are, because
they are part of the Hebrew text; Psalm 119's acrostic letters are not,
because they are a printer's aid.

## The voice

Synthesised, not a person. It is one consistent narrator across the whole
Bible, generated locally and checked by ear book by book.
